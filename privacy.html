MoneyMint Scheduler – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

MoneyMint Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of the MoneyMintSheets shop to publish the owner's own product Pins to the owner's own Pinterest account on a schedule. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app uses

  • An OAuth access token for the owner's Pinterest account, stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub Actions settings.
  • The names and IDs of the owner's own boards, used only to choose where each Pin is published.
  • The IDs of Pins the app has published, saved so the same Pin is not published twice.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, read, store or share data about any other Pinterest users.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use Pinterest data for advertising or for training any models.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings (Security and logins → Apps). Stored tokens are deleted when the app is disconnected.
